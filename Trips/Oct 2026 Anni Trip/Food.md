# Food

##### ॐ श्री आशुतोषाय नमः


Simple Indian vegetarian food for six people for about 30 days (180 person-days). Quantities include about 10 per cent spare. Dry staples are carried; vegetables, milk and fruit are bought in Anni each week.

#### Meals

- Breakfast: tea, with poha, upma, dalia or parathas.
- Lunch: dal, rice, roti, one sabzi.
- Dinner: roti, dal or rajma or chana, one sabzi; khichdi on busy days.
- Snacks: roasted chana, peanuts, jaggery, biscuits.

#### Buy Each Week in Anni

- Potatoes 7 kg, onions 5 kg, tomatoes 3 kg, ginger, garlic, green chillies.
- Green vegetables and fruit for the week.
- Milk, if available, about 3 L a day.

#### Storage

- Staples in airtight drums or tins with lids, off the ground, covered by tarpaulin: safe from damp, rats and monkeys.
- Onions and potatoes in open sacks, somewhere dry and airy.
- Oil and ghee sealed and away from the stove.

#### Cooking Fuel and Stove

- Two-burner LPG stove with regulator and pipe.
- Two 14.2 kg LPG cylinders: about three weeks each for six people cooking three meals and boiling some water; the second is the spare.
- Firewood, where allowed, for heating bath water and saving gas.

#### Water

- Source: whatever the camp has (tap, spring or stream); all of it is treated as unsafe until filtered or boiled.
- Cleaning: a gravity filter for all drinking and cooking water; boil when in doubt; purification tablets as backup.
- Daily need: about 30 L for drinking and cooking (5 L a person), and about 60 to 90 L for washing and bathing (10 to 15 L a person).
- Storage: four 20 L cans of clean water (80 L), enough for more than two days of drinking and cooking; buckets for washing water.
