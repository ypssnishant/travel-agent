# Packing List

##### ॐ श्री आशुतोषाय नमः


For six people, a month at Anni in October: warm days, cold nights that get colder through the month. Numbers are for the whole group.

#### Shelter and Bedding

- [ ] Tarpaulins, heavy, 3 (roof leaks, floor, cover for supplies)
- [ ] Ground sheets, 2 large
- [ ] Sleeping bags rated to 0°C or thick quilts, 6
- [ ] Mattresses or foam mats, 6
- [ ] Blankets, 6 (one extra layer each for late October)
- [ ] Pillows, 6
- [ ] Rope, 30 m, and tent pegs

#### Water

- [ ] Gravity water filter, 1
- [ ] Purification tablets, 2 strips (backup)
- [ ] Water cans, 20 L, 4
- [ ] Water bottles, 6
- [ ] Buckets, 3, and mugs, 3
- [ ] Large pot for boiling water

#### Kitchen

- [ ] LPG stove (2 burner) with regulator and pipe
- [ ] LPG cylinders, 2
- [ ] Matches and lighters, kept in two places
- [ ] Pressure cooker, 5 L
- [ ] Pots, 2 large, kadhai, tawa, lids
- [ ] Ladles, spatula, knives, 2, chopping board
- [ ] Plates, bowls, glasses, spoons, 6 sets
- [ ] Airtight drums and containers for staples (rats and monkeys)
- [ ] Washing-up liquid, scrubbers, cloths
- [ ] Garbage bags, 1 roll per week

#### Power and Light

- [ ] Head torches, 6
- [ ] Rechargeable or solar lanterns, 2
- [ ] Spare batteries for the torches, a month's worth
- [ ] Power banks, 3 (20,000 mAh)
- [ ] Solar panel for charging, if there's no electricity
- [ ] Charging cables and an extension board

#### Health and First Aid

- [ ] First-aid kit for six
- [ ] Each person's regular medicines for five weeks
- [ ] Paracetamol and a pain reliever
- [ ] Stomach and diarrhoea medicine
- [ ] Oral rehydration sachets, 20
- [ ] Cold, cough and allergy medicine
- [ ] Antiseptic, bandages, cotton, plasters, crepe bandage
- [ ] Burn cream
- [ ] Thermometer
- [ ] Moisturiser, sunscreen and lip balm (dry, sunny days)
- [ ] Insect repellent

#### Hygiene and Toilet

- [ ] Soap, shampoo, toothpaste for a month
- [ ] Toothbrushes and towels, 6
- [ ] Hand sanitiser, 3
- [ ] Toilet paper, 24 rolls
- [ ] Sanitary items, if needed
- [ ] Toilet tent and spade, if there's no toilet
- [ ] Washing powder, clothes line and pegs

#### Clothes (each person)

- [ ] Thermal top and bottom, 2 sets
- [ ] Sweater or fleece, 2
- [ ] Warm jacket
- [ ] Rain jacket or poncho
- [ ] Woollen cap and gloves
- [ ] Woollen socks, 3 pairs, and cotton socks
- [ ] Everyday clothes for a week, washed weekly
- [ ] Sturdy shoes
- [ ] Slippers

#### Tools and Repairs

- [ ] Multi-tool or knife
- [ ] Hammer, nails, wire
- [ ] Axe or saw (firewood)
- [ ] Spade
- [ ] Duct tape and cable ties
- [ ] Sewing kit

#### Phone and Network

- [ ] Phones with Jio and BSNL or Airtel SIMs across the group
- [ ] Offline maps of Kullu district downloaded
- [ ] Key numbers on paper (Civil Hospital Ani, family, the local contact)

#### Documents and Money

- [ ] Aadhaar or other ID for all six
- [ ] Permission letter for the stay, if any
- [ ] Copies of documents
- [ ] Cash for the month
- [ ] Emergency contact list

#### Safety

- [ ] Whistle, 2
- [ ] Sand bucket near the stove
- [ ] Walking sticks
