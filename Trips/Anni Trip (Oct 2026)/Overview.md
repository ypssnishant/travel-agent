#### The Trip

- Anni Trip, a seva trip.
- Where: Anni (also spelled Ani), Kullu district, Himachal Pradesh.
- When: 2 Oct 2026 to about 1 Nov 2026, about a month.
- Who: six people.
- Stay: a makeshift camp or a very basic house, where nothing is available.

#### The Place

- Anni is the tehsil and sub-division headquarters of Outer Seraj, in the south of Kullu district, on the Satluj side of the Jalori Pass.
- Height: about 1,227 m (Anni town). Villages around it sit higher or lower; a camp higher up is colder.
- Road: National Highway 305 runs Sainj (on NH-5) – Luhri – Anni – Jalori Pass – Banjar – Aut (on NH-3), 97 km in all. Luhri to Anni is about 20 km.
- Jalori Pass (over 3,000 m) on the Kullu side closes for about four months in winter because of snow; it's normally open in October.
- HRTC buses run to Anni.

#### Weather

- Early October forecast for Anni town (yr.no, as of 30 Sep 2026): 25 to 28°C by day, 14 to 16°C at night, mostly clear, some rain around 6 and 7 October.
- October is when winter begins in Kullu district: nights and mornings turn cold and get colder through the month.

#### What It Has

- Hospital: Civil Hospital, Ani (Sarahan, PIN 172102).
- Market: Anni town is the local market for Outer Seraj.
- Mobile network: Jio, Airtel and BSNL all work in Kullu district, with government roaming sites in remote parts; coverage at the camp itself is not known.
