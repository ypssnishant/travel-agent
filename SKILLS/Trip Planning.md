# Trip Planning

##### ॐ श्री आशुतोषाय नमः


How the Travel Agent plans any trip, especially a stay where nothing is available (a camp, a basic house). Work through every section for every trip; a section that doesn't apply is skipped, not left out by accident.

#### Start

- Read `Lessons.md` for what past trips taught.
- Get the facts: where, dates, how long, how many people, what kind of stay, why (seva or personal).
- Look up the place on the web: height, weather for the exact dates (day and night, start and end of the stay), nearest town and market, hospital, mobile network, road access and closures. Write it into the trip's `Overview.md`, with the sources.
- Anything not found or not known is asked of Kulwinder, never guessed.
- Create the trip folder and its five files, then plan each section below into them.

#### Shelter and bedding

- What the stay has: roof, walls, floor, doors, space for how many.
- Tents or tarpaulins if the shelter is poor or leaks; ground sheets for any floor.
- For each person: sleeping bag or quilt rated for the coldest night, mat or mattress, pillow, blanket.
- Plan for the coldest night of the stay, not the average.

#### Water

- Where drinking water comes from (tap, spring, stream, tanker) and how far.
- How it's cleaned: boiling, filter, purification tablets; always a second method as backup.
- How much: 4 to 5 litres per person per day for drinking and cooking, more for washing.
- Storage: enough clean containers for at least two days.

#### Food and cooking

- Menu for the whole stay, planned by day or by week, fitted to the group's diet.
- Quantities of staples per person per day, multiplied out, plus 10 per cent spare.
- What can be bought locally and how often, and what must be carried.
- Storage safe from damp, rats, insects and monkeys: airtight tins and drums.
- Stove and fuel for the whole stay (LPG, kerosene, wood), matches and lighters in two places.
- Utensils, pressure cooker, washing up.

#### Power and light

- Whether there's electricity, and how reliable.
- Head torches for everyone, lanterns for common spaces, spare batteries.
- Power banks, a solar panel or charging plan for phones.

#### Health and first aid

- Nearest hospital and chemist, and how to get there in an emergency.
- A first-aid kit sized for the group and the length of stay.
- Each person's own regular medicines for the whole stay plus a week.
- Medicines for fever, pain, stomach, cold and cough, allergy, cuts and burns, and oral rehydration.

#### Hygiene and toilets

- What toilet there is; if none, a dug pit, a toilet tent, spades and lime or ash.
- Washing: buckets, mugs, heating water for bathing in the cold.
- Soap, sanitiser, toilet paper, sanitary items, waste bags; carry out what can't be burnt or buried.

#### Clothes for the weather

- Layers for the coldest night and the warmest day: base layers, fleece or sweater, jacket, cap, gloves, woollen socks.
- Rain gear if rain is possible.
- Sturdy shoes for the ground, and slippers for camp.
- Enough changes for the washing plan.

#### Tools and repairs

- Knife, rope, tape, tarp, hammer, nails, wire, spade, axe or saw if wood is used.
- Sewing kit, repair tape, spare tent pegs, cable ties.

#### Phone and network

- Which networks work at the place; carry SIMs of two networks where possible.
- Offline maps, key numbers written on paper.
- Where the nearest signal is if the stay has none.

#### Documents and money

- ID for everyone, any permission letters, emergency contacts.
- Cash for the stay: ATMs may be far or empty.
- Copies of documents, in print and on the phone.

#### Travel there and back

- Mode (own vehicle, hired vehicle, bus), route, road condition and closures for the season.
- Load plan: what fits in the vehicle for the whole group and all the supplies.
- Return date and how the return is arranged.

#### Safety

- Who is told where the group is and when it returns.
- Weather risks (landslides, rain, snow), wild animals, fire safety in the camp.
- A plan for an emergency: who drives, where to, what number to call.

#### Before leaving

- Every `#### Buy` item bought and every `#### To Do` item done in `Tasks.md`.
- Packing list ticked off as the bags are packed.
- Budget checked against cash in hand.
