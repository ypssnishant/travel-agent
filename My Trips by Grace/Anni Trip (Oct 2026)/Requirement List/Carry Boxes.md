#### The Boxes

##### 1. Tea Box (first night)

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Small, 25 L; tea is made about 18 times a week, all from one box)</span>

- [ ] Chai patti (1 box)
- [ ] Khand jar
- [ ] Milk powder tin
- [ ] Elaichi
- [ ] Saunf
- [ ] Coffee pouches
- [ ] Electric kettle
- [ ] Biscuits, rusk or namkeen (a week's)

##### 2. Tea Refill Box

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Medium, 40 L; weekly top-up for No. 1)</span>

- [ ] Biscuits, rusk and namkeen (the rest)
- [ ] Khand (the rest)
- [ ] Milk powder (the rest)
- [ ] Chai patti (second box)

##### 3. Breakfast Box

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Small, 25 L; sandwiches, poha and peanut salad)</span>

- [ ] Peanut butter
- [ ] Jam
- [ ] Sandwich sauces (red and white)
- [ ] Grater
- [ ] Poha
- [ ] Peanuts

##### 4. Masala Box (first night)

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Small, 25 L; every meal uses it alongside its own box)</span>

- [ ] All masala jars
- [ ] Namak and kala namak
- [ ] Hing
- [ ] Tej patta
- [ ] Kali mirch
- [ ] Oregano and chilli flakes
- [ ] Soya sauce and vinegar

##### 5. Dal, Rice and Chana Box

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Medium, 40 L; all the pulses and grains together)</span>

- [ ] Rice (10 kg)
- [ ] Urd dhuli, moong dhuli and arhar dal
- [ ] Rajma
- [ ] White chana and black chana

##### 6. Lunch and Pasta Box (first night)

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Large, 55–60 L, about 60 × 40 × 35 cm; the cooking extras for lunch and dinner; Maggi is the quick first dinner)</span>

- [ ] Besan
- [ ] Dry bundi
- [ ] Dry pakora
- [ ] Soya chunks
- [ ] Macaroni (5 kg)
- [ ] Atta Maggi (20)
- [ ] Strainer

##### 7. Oil and Ketchup Tub

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(15 L plastic tub with lid and high sides, about 35 × 25 × 30 cm, lined with newspaper or an old cloth; a leak stays in the tub and can't spoil food; the can travels upright)</span>

- [ ] Mustard oil (5 L can)
- [ ] Ketchup
- [ ] Achar

##### 8. Appliances Box

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Small, 25 L; each in its own carton or wrapped in a cloth)</span>

- [ ] Electric sandwich maker
- [ ] Hand blender

##### 9. Dishes Box

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Small, 25 L; used after every meal, kept away from food)</span>

- [ ] Dishwash soap
- [ ] Scrub
- [ ] Kitchen cloth
- [ ] Napkins
- [ ] Disposals

##### 10. Atta Drum

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(50 L drum with tight lid, about 40 cm across × 50 cm tall; heavy, sealed against damp and rats)</span>

- [ ] Wheat atta (30 kg)

##### 11. Vegetable Bag

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Jute bag, holds about 10 kg; needs air, not a closed box)</span>

- [ ] Aloo (7 kg, first week)
- [ ] Adrak (first week)

##### 12. Medicine Box (first night)

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(8–10 L first aid box, about 30 × 20 × 18 cm; quick to find at any hour)</span>

- [ ] All medicines and first aid

##### 13. Electricals Box (first night)

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Small, 25 L; light and charging on arrival, wires together)</span>

- [ ] Power banks
- [ ] Chargers and charging cables
- [ ] Multi-plug and extension boards
- [ ] Power adapters
- [ ] Torches
- [ ] Clothing iron

##### 14. Bucket in the Dustbin (first night)

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(20 L bucket with mug, inside a 35–40 L dustbin with lid, about 40 cm across × 50 cm tall; uses empty space; toilet and room get cleaned the first night)</span>

- [ ] Phenyl and toilet brush
- [ ] Liquid handwash and soap
- [ ] Wiping cloths
- [ ] Garbage bags
- [ ] Dustpan
- [ ] Laundry detergent and cloth cleaning brush
- [ ] Clothes pegs and hangers
- [ ] Clothesline

##### 15. Utility Box (first night)

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Small, 25 L; occasional-use things in one place; padlocks and matchbox needed the first night)</span>

- [ ] Toolkit
- [ ] Nylon rope, duct tape and cable ties
- [ ] Padlocks
- [ ] Matchbox
- [ ] Arti speaker
- [ ] Pen and notebook

##### 16. Bedding Bag (first night)

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(Jumbo zip-up bag, 100–150 L; soft and bulky, rides on top of the boxes)</span>

- [ ] Pillows
- [ ] Bedsheets
- [ ] Razai / Kambal
- [ ] Sleeping bags
- [ ] Gadde (tied on, if carried)

##### Personal Bags (first night)

<span style="color: var(--text-faint)">(One per person; nobody digs in the shared boxes)</span>

- [ ] Clothing
- [ ] Toiletries
- [ ] Own medicines
- [ ] Water bottle
- [ ] Sadhna asan and sadhna blanket or shawl
